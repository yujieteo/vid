# The Blue-Eyed Islanders puzzle (video 2)

Goal: teach the puzzle, the answer, and why the visitor's remark matters.

## Sources (read on 2026-10-20)
- Terry Tao, "The blue-eyed islanders puzzle" (2008): https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2008/02/05/the-blue-eyed-islanders-puzzle/
- Robert Heaton, "The Blue-Eyed Islanders": https://robertheaton.com/2014/01/06/how-to-win-at-dinner-party-the-blue-eyed-islanders/

## Facts used
- (Tao) Each islander sees all other eye colors, never their own. Talking about eye color is forbidden.
- (Tao) A person who learns their own eye color must act at noon the next day. 100 have blue eyes, 900 brown.
  The islanders do not know these numbers. They are perfectly logical, and they know that the others are.
- (Tao) A trusted blue-eyed visitor says it is unusual to see another blue-eyed person here.
- (Tao) Argument 1: the remark is nothing new. Argument 2: with n blue-eyed people, all n act on day n.
  The induction: n=1 acts on day 1. For n>1, each waits for day n-1. If nothing happens, each knows their own color.
- (Heaton) The remark makes the fact common knowledge. Each day of silence adds one level. Two people: day 2.

## Change from the source
- Tao's puzzle ends with ritual suicide. This video says "must leave the island". The logic is the same.

## Not in the sources
- Tao's post does not give the solution (he leaves it to the comments). The explanation of the two arguments
  comes from Heaton. The case n=2 is Tao's induction step with n=2.
