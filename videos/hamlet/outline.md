# Hamlet in 15 minutes

Goal: tell the whole story of Shakespeare's Hamlet, from the ghost to the end. About 15 minutes.

## Source (read on 2026-10-20)
- William Shakespeare, "The Tragedy of Hamlet, Prince of Denmark". Project Gutenberg eBook #1524 (public domain): https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1524
- The play is the only source. The video states only what the play text says or shows.
- Each quoted line is exact and short. Each quote was checked against the text.

## Facts used (act and scene in the play)
- I.1 Night, on the wall of Elsinore. Barnardo, Marcellus and Horatio see a ghost that looks like the dead King. It goes when the cock crows. They decide to tell Hamlet.
- I.2 Claudius is King. He married Gertrude, Hamlet's mother, within a month of the old King's death. Hamlet wears black. Laertes goes back to France. Horatio tells Hamlet about the ghost.
- I.3 Laertes warns Ophelia about Hamlet. Polonius gives advice to Laertes and orders Ophelia to stop her talks with Hamlet.
- I.4-5 The Ghost says it is Hamlet's father. A serpent did not kill it: Claudius poured poison in its ear when it slept in the orchard. It asks for revenge. Hamlet swears Horatio and Marcellus to secrecy. Hamlet may "put an antic disposition on".
- II.1 Polonius sends Reynaldo to spy on Laertes. Ophelia tells how Hamlet came to her, pale. Polonius thinks it is love madness.
- II.2 Claudius and Gertrude call Rosencrantz and Guildenstern. The ambassadors from Norway say old Norway stopped Fortinbras: he will go to Poland. Polonius reads a love letter of Hamlet. Hamlet calls Polonius a fishmonger. Hamlet says Denmark is a prison. He knows that R and G were sent for. Players come. Hamlet asks them to play "The Murder of Gonzago" with lines of his.
- III.1 "To be, or not to be". Ophelia gives back the gifts. Hamlet: "Get thee to a nunnery." Claudius and Polonius watch from behind. Claudius says that Hamlet's trouble is not love, and sends him to England.
- III.2 Hamlet asks Horatio to watch the King. The play shows a man who pours poison in a sleeper's ears. The King stands up and calls for light. Hamlet will take the Ghost's word.
- III.3 Claudius tries to pray. He keeps the crown, the ambition and the Queen, so he cannot. Hamlet could kill him, but decides not to kill a man at prayer.
- III.4 In the Queen's room, Polonius hides behind the arras. Hamlet stabs through it and kills Polonius. The Ghost comes back. Gertrude cannot see it.
- IV.1-3 Claudius sends Hamlet to England with Rosencrantz and Guildenstern. Hamlet hides the body. "At supper." Claudius sends a letter to England: it asks for the death of Hamlet.
- IV.4 Hamlet sees the army of Fortinbras on its way to Poland. He decides that his thoughts must be "bloody".
- IV.5 Ophelia sings. She gives out flowers. Laertes comes in with armed men and asks for his father.
- IV.6 Sailors bring a letter from Hamlet to Horatio. A pirate ship took Hamlet. He is back. Rosencrantz and Guildenstern go on to England.
- IV.7 Claudius gets a letter from Hamlet: "naked" in Denmark. Claudius and Laertes plan a fencing match: Laertes will use a sharp sword with poison. Claudius will have a poisoned cup. The Queen says that Ophelia fell into the brook and drowned.
- V.1 Two gravediggers. A skull is Yorick's, the King's jester. The funeral of Ophelia: the Queen scatters flowers. Laertes jumps into the grave. Hamlet jumps in too. They fight.
- V.2 Hamlet tells Horatio: on the ship he took the letter, wrote a new one, and sealed it with his father's signet. R and G are dead. Osric brings the invitation. Hamlet: "The readiness is all."
- V.2 The match. Hamlet hits twice. Gertrude drinks from the cup. Laertes wounds Hamlet. They change swords and Hamlet wounds Laertes. Gertrude dies. Laertes tells Hamlet the plan and that the King is to blame. Hamlet stabs the King and makes him drink. Laertes asks for forgiveness and dies. Hamlet asks Horatio to tell his story and says that Fortinbras gets his vote. Hamlet dies. Fortinbras comes. Horatio says he will tell all. Fortinbras orders honours for Hamlet.

## Not in the video
- Facts that the play does not state (for example, Hamlet's age, the year, how long the story takes). The video does not give them.
- The long speeches in full. The video quotes short lines only.
- Scenes with no part in the story line (for example, the long talk about the child actors).

## Scenes (about 40 s each, 23 scenes, about 16 minutes)
1 Title and cast. 2 The ghost on the wall. 3 The new King. 4 Hamlet hears. 5 The Ghost speaks. 6 The oath. 7 The madness. 8 The players. 9 To be or not to be. 10 The nunnery. 11 The Mousetrap. 12 The prayer. 13 The Queen's room. 14 The body. 15 Ophelia sings. 16 The pirates. 17 The plot. 18 The gravediggers. 19 The funeral. 20 The new letter. 21 The match. 22 The end. 23 Close.

## Cut for length
The first draft had 28 scenes and ran 19.2 minutes. These 5 scenes were cut: Laertes and Polonius (I.3), Polonius spies (II.1), Rosencrantz and Guildenstern (II.2), The plan (III.2) and The army (IV.4).
